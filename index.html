Tucked into six wooded acres fifteen minutes from Mount Hood's Timberline Lodge, this cedar cabin was built by hand in 2019 and sleeps four across two bedrooms. Mornings start on the covered porch with coffee and mountain air; evenings end in the private hot tub under the trees. The cabin runs on high-speed fiber internet, so it suits a quiet work trip as easily as a weekend away. Inside you'll find a wood-burning stove, a fully equipped kitchen, and windows on three sides that keep the forest close all day long.
